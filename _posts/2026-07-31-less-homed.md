We were in bands together from like thirteen to sixteen. Smoked pot together. Tried angel dust and acid for the first time. Played a shit ton of D&D. Pot had seeds in it back then, George recently pointed out in a text message. His day job isn't enough.

And ef you see kay.

Omar who was for the last decade and a half in the white house across the street and two blocks east is now sleeping in the parking lot of the church, hoping he can find a place to rent for four hundred a month, or buy a car to sleep in.

You might think most people have money in their bank accounts. Don't think that.

Times are tough. Fucking tough.
